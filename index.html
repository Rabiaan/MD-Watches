At MD Watches, founded and operated by Danial Saeed & Muneeb Anjum, we believe a timepiece is much more than a device for telling time. It is a defining statement of discipline, refined taste, and personal presentation.

Based at Nawabpur Road, Nawab City, Punjab, our studio curates an exceptional range of sport timepieces, classic dress watches, and skeleton mechanical calibres. Each model in our catalog is hand-inspected for bezel alignment, dial finish, hand positioning, and bracelet fluidity before being securely packaged and dispatched across Pakistan.
